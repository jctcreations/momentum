Goals & settings

How you progress

Every 8 workouts you move up a level (1 through 5). Each level does two things: your reps and hold times go up, and a few exercises are swapped for fresh, slightly tougher moves — so the workouts keep evolving instead of getting stale.

Add to your home screen

Open this page in your phone's browser, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It'll open like a real app.
